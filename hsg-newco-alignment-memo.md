# HSG NewCo — Founding CEO Alignment Memo

**Purpose:** Align on the business structure and operating relationship.

## 1. Company structure and team

### Proposed structure

| Item | Proposal |
|---|---|
| HSG Managing Partners | 60% equity |
| Founding CEO | 25% equity, subject to vesting |
| Employee pool | 15% ESOP |
| Reporting | CEO reports to HSG NewCo Board/Managing Partners |
| Operations | CEO controls day-to-day operations within the agreed plan |
| Hiring | Contract-first; full-time hiring as backlog and cash flow develop |

### CEO responsibility

The CEO is responsible for:

- Product roadmap and business priorities.
- Building and managing the team.
- Successful project delivery and customer acceptance.
- Establishing post-delivery support.
- Building recurring state business.
- Owning the complete NewCo P&L.

### Team and skills

| Role | Location | Responsibility | Initial model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding CEO | US | P&L, product roadmap, sales, deals, delivery, staffing and customers | Full-time, W2 Employee with benefits   |
| CTO,if he joins     | Canada | Architecture, AI workflows, engineering standards, security and technical hiring | Full-time if the candidate joins |
| Production Systems Integration Engineer | US | Production systems, integrations, APIs, deployment and customer technical coordination | Fractional initially |
| Engineering Lead | India | Technical leadership, estimation, delivery planning and team management | Contract initially |
| Full-stack Engineers | India | Application and integration delivery | Contract by project |
| AI/ML Engineer | India | AI workflows, evaluation and model integration | Contract by project |
| Data/Knowledge Engineer | India | Data and knowledge pipelines | Contract by project |
| QA Engineer | India | Functional, regression and automated testing | Contract by project |
| DevOps/SRE | India | Deployment, reliability and support | Fractional contract |
| Implementation Analyst | India | Customer configuration and implementation | Contract by project |
| Program & Delivery Manager | US | Delivery governance, financial tracking and escalation | Fractional initially |
| Customer Success/FDE | US | Customer support, renewal and expansion | Added at go-live |

If the Canada CTO does not join, the India Engineering Lead reports directly to the CEO and the U.S. Production Systems Integration Engineer provides fractional technical support.

Contract roles move to full-time as recurring requirements, utilization, backlog and cash flow support the decision.

### CEO equity vesting

The Founding CEO's 25% equity will vest over a **three-year period** through a combination of:

- **Time-based vesting** to reflect continued leadership and commitment to building NewCo.
- **Performance-based vesting** to align equity with agreed business and execution outcomes.

The allocation between the two components, timing within the three-year schedule, milestones and other terms remain to be discussed and finalized with HSG.

## 2. CEO ownership of the business

### P&L ownership

The CEO owns the complete NewCo P&L, including:

- Revenue, pricing, project economics and gross margin.
- Operating expenses, cash flow and working capital.
- Annual budget and rolling financial forecast.
- Hiring and contractor decisions based on requirements and cash flow.
- Invoicing, collections and project profitability.
- Separate NewCo books and commercial arrangements with HSG Consulting.
- Quarterly business reporting and future capital requirements.

### Quarterly reporting

| Area | Reporting |
|---|---|
| Sales | Pipeline, bookings, forecast and partnerships |
| Financial | Revenue, margin, expenses, cash and runway |
| Customers | Delivery, collections, renewals and risks |
| People | Team, utilization and hiring requirements |
| Outlook | Next-quarter plan and decisions required |

### Sales involvement

Work with Managing Partners and the HSG team for sales development:

- Account strategy and ground-level relationship development.
- Customer discovery, qualification, scoping and pricing.
- Partnerships with Technology vendors like AWS, Adobe, OpenAI/Anthropic
- Proposals, negotiation, closing, renewal and expansion.
- Public-sector procurement paths and partner structures.
- Direct-prime, HSG-prime/NewCo-subcontract, teaming, referral, channel and other sales vehicles.


## 3. Managing Partners investment, cash policy and bonus

### Initial Managing Partners investment

The initial funding requirement will be developed with HSG based on:

1. **Launch funding:** CEO compensation, benefits, company formation, insurance, finance, systems, sales,marketing and core operating costs.
2. **Project funding:** Contract delivery teams, customer support and project expenses.
3. **Working capital:** Cash needed between payroll, project spending and customer collections.
4. **Technical leadership:** Canada CTO costs or the fractional U.S. engineering alternative.
5. **Contingency:** An agreed operating and delivery reserve.

The parties will agree the initial amount, runway and funding schedule after reviewing the launch plan and first-project requirements. Once agreed, the Managing Partners commitment will not create additional dilution.

Debt may be used to bridge receivables or project-start costs. The CEO will not provide a personal guarantee, 

### CEO Year 1 bonus

The Year 1 bonus rewards successful delivery, customer acceptance, project profitability and collections.

> **CEO Bonus = 10% × Collected Project Gross Profit**

**Collected Project Gross Profit** is:

> Cash collected from delivered and customer-accepted projects  
> **less** direct project-delivery costs

Direct costs include delivery labor, project contractors, project infrastructure, project travel and other costs required to deliver the work. Corporate overhead and company-formation costs do not reduce project gross profit.

| Collected project gross profit | CEO bonus |
|---:|---:|
| $250,000 | $25,000 |
| $500,000 | $50,000 |
| $1,000,000 | $100,000 |
| $2,000,000 | $200,000 |

There is no fixed bonus cap. The Year 2 bonus structure will be agreed by July 31 based on the Year 2 plan.

Should cash remain in NewCo to fund operations, delivery, growth, working capital and other obligations, with shareholder distributions made pro rata only once the business has sufficient operating cash? HSG may be paid separately for agreed services provided to NewCo.
